### 1. Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO — markets explode
- 11 days after resigning as CEO, Jensen Huang returned to run Nvidia on

  S&P 500 +4.7%, chip index +12%. Reuters calls it "one of the most

### 2. Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya
- Zelensky confirmed Ukrainian forces used 7 naval drones to sink a Russian
  warship at the port of Benghazi, Libya — the war reaching the
  Mediterranean. A merchant ship was also struck; a Russian cargo ship with
  military ties, the Sparta II, caught fire Sat 22 Nov in the Gulf of
  Finland after a drone strike Finnish officials said was likely Ukraine's.
- Sources: Dow Jones via Morningstar 23 Nov; Principia 23 Nov digest
  (principia.news/e/2026-11-23); Off the Wire 23 Nov.

### 3. COP31 climate talks collapse — no deal
- The COP31 conference in Antalya, Turkey, collapsed on Sun 22 Nov after
  2+ weeks of talks and 50+ hours overtime — the longest COP in history
  and the first major breakdown since COP15 Copenhagen 2009. The "Antalya
  Pact" was rejected because the final text did not mention fossil fuels;
  194 delegations, consensus required. EU, Panama, Marshall Islands,
  Norway among those rejecting it; Russia and Saudi Arabia opposed any
  fossil-fuel language. US absent (Trump pulled out of international
  climate action). UN chief Guterres "deeply disappointed".
- Sources: CNN 22 Nov; AP via Barchart 23 Nov; WSJ 23 Nov; Le Monde 22 Nov;
  Phys.org 22 Nov. (Collapse was Sunday; it led Monday's news.)

## Medium stories

### 4. Indonesia candy-factory fire — 24 dead, ~30 missing
- Fire broke out Sat 21 Nov ~4:30–5pm at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia
  confectionery factory in Cikande, Serang Regency, Banten. On Mon 23 Nov
  Indonesia confirmed 24 dead; about 30 people still missing (some
  reports say 34). ~300 people were on site for the Saturday shift; 29
  confirmed safe, 5 in hospital. Preliminary cause: air-conditioner
  short circuit (under investigation). The factory makes Milkita candy.
- Sources: Reuters via DailyFoxNews 23 Nov; Wikipedia "2026 Serang Regency
  factory fire"; PEOPLE 23 Nov.

### 9. Belgium national strike grounds flights — Mon 23 Nov
- Air traffic controllers (skeyes) joined the national union strike against
  PM Bart De Wever's austerity measures. Brussels Airport cancelled ALL
  arrivals and departures 07:00–21:00 local (14-hour window); ~429 flights
  and ~59,500–60,000 passengers scheduled that day. Brussels South
  Charleroi Airport halted flights for the whole day; Liège ~70 flights
  affected. Unions: ACV-CSC, ABVV-FGTB, ACLVB; ~60% of controllers took
  part. Announced Sat 21 Nov, <36 hours' notice.
- Sources: Airways Magazine 23 Nov; TravelWires 23 Nov; The Brussels Times
  via Belga; airlate.com (facts checked 23 Nov 2026).

### 10. Democrats clinch 258 House seats — largest since 1992
- House Democrats won at least 258 seats (+44), the largest House majority
  for either party since 1992 (Clinton's first election, also 258; Obama
  2008 was 257). The 258th seat was reached Sun 22 Nov after Democrat Chuck
  Hubbard defeated Rep. Virginia Foxx (NC-5, recount). Republicans at
  least 173. Democrats take control of both chambers on 3 Jan 2027.
  (Sunday milestone; led Monday's news.)
- Sources: AP 22 Nov (republished 23 Nov); Fox News 23 Nov; The Times.
