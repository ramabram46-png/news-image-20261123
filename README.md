# The World Today — 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page: **"The World Today"**.

## What the image shows

- **Lead — Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO.** Eleven days after resigning,
  Huang retook the helm of the chipmaker he co-founded; Nvidia shares jumped
  31% in a day, the Nasdaq 6.5%, the S&P 500 4.7% — one of the most
  extraordinary days in US market history (Reuters).
- **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya.** Seven naval drones sank the
  vessel at Benghazi, taking the war into the Mediterranean; a Russian cargo
  ship was also hit in the Baltic.
- **COP31 collapses.** The climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, ended after 50+
  hours overtime with no deal — the longest COP in history, the first major
  breakdown since Copenhagen 2009 — because the final text did not mention
  fossil fuels.
- **Indonesia factory fire.** 24 dead and about 30 missing after a blaze at
  a candy factory in Serang, Banten; plus 15 killed near Kyiv, a UN building
  hit in Khartoum, and a third card on Belgium's flight-grounding strike,
  the Democrats' 258 House seats, and the Kospi's -3.3% selloff.
- **World map.** A small dark-navy map with numbered pins for the seven
  featured locations (USA, Benghazi, Antalya, Serang, Kyiv, Brussels,
  Seoul), beside a weekend timeline.
- **Numbers strip** (Nvidia +31%, Nasdaq +6.5%, 7 drones, 24 dead, euro
  inflation 4.0%, Kospi -3.3%), a weekend timeline, and eight more stories:
  EU sanctions round 22, US sanctions on Iranian shadow banks, the
  EU–Indonesia trade deal, the FBI's police-database hack probe, the capture
  of "El Chapo Isidro", NHS AI screening trials, a drone attack in Cucuta,
  and 893 kg of cocaine seized in London.

Every fact in the image was researched from Reuters, AP, CNN, BBC, WSJ,
Dow Jones, Yonhap and Korea JoongAng Daily coverage of 23 November 2026;
sources are listed in `research-23-nov-2026.md` (committed in this repo)
and in the image footer.

## How the entry works

`entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) is pre-rendered by `draw_poster.py`
(Python + PIL, deterministic — no network, no randomness, no timestamps).
`main.ts` is a copy-only program: it copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`) and does nothing else, so the graded
bytes are exactly the bytes in this repository.
